# NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Recorded: YYYY-MM-DD
- Scope: Component or cross-cutting boundary
- Supersedes: None
- Superseded by: None

## Context and constraints

Describe the concrete problem, relevant existing behavior, and constraints driving
the choice. State what is verified and what is assumed. For a retrospective
record, say so; do not invent historical dates, participants, or approvals.

## Decision

State the chosen approach, ownership/boundaries, and the behavior it commits to.
Separate existing implementation from planned work or unresolved details.

## Alternatives

List credible alternatives and explain why the chosen approach better satisfies
the stated constraints. For retrospective records, describe present trade-offs
rather than implying historical consideration without evidence.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, limitations, and obligations that follow from the
decision. Include relevant safety, performance, operability, and maintenance
effects. Do not replace concrete trade-offs with a generic checklist.

## Verification and references

Link relevant source, tests, related ADRs, and current documentation. Explain what
evidence would verify the decision and identify remaining integration work. Only
report completed validation when it was actually run; prefer linking tests over
embedding a test-run snapshot that will go stale.
